Our Charter

What we stand for

Fellowship

The Club exists first as a room worth walking into — a standing table for members to meet, trade ideas, and keep good company on a regular footing.

Standards

Membership is not a formality. We hold ourselves to a shared code of conduct, courtesy, and craft, and expect the same of every member who joins us.

Occasion

From quarterly assemblies to smaller evening gatherings, the calendar is built around real reasons to meet — never obligation for its own sake.

Continuity

The Club is built to outlast any one member's tenure. Traditions are recorded, roles are handed down, and the archive grows with every season.

“A club is not a building. It is an agreement, kept, between people who choose each other.”

For Whom

Who we seek

Membership is selective by design — we look for a particular kind of person, not a particular résumé.

The Builder

Serious about their work. Founders, operators, and makers who take their craft seriously and want company that does the same.

The Steward

Serious about their standards. Those who keep their word, show up on time, and understand that reputation is built in small moments.

The Quiet Achiever

Serious about the work, not the spotlight. People comfortable letting results speak, who'd rather be useful in a small room than loud in a large one.

Privileges

What membership includes

Private Assemblies

Quarterly gatherings and smaller evenings, reserved for members and their guests.

Curated Introductions

Access to a directory of peers, and introductions made with intention, not volume.

A Room to Speak Freely

Discretion is enforced by the Charter, so conversation can stay honest.

Priority Reservations

First access to seats at ticketed assemblies and dinners, ahead of general release.

The Archive

A running record of the Club's gatherings, traditions, and members past and present.

Guest Privileges

Questions

Before you apply

How is membership reviewed?+

Applications are read personally, not filtered by an algorithm. We look for fit with the Charter first, credentials second.

Can I attend an assembly before joining?+

Occasionally, by invitation from an existing member. There is no general public open house.

Are membership payments refundable?+

Membership is reviewed and confirmed after payment and application are submitted together. If you have a specific concern, reach out via the Request Membership section.

How is discretion actually enforced?+

By the Charter, and by the fact that members who breach it are asked to leave. It is a norm we take seriously, not a slogan.

Can I upgrade or change my tier later?+

Yes — submit a new application under Request Membership for the new tier, with payment, and it will be reviewed the same way.

Do you host events outside major cities?+

The calendar is centered where the membership is densest today, and grows as the Club does. Check the Calendar for what's upcoming.
